# HinduMania Privacy Policy

**App:** HinduMania (`com.mak_cell.hindumania`)
**Developer:** MakCell
**Last updated:** 25 September 2026

HinduMania is a devotional app for reading, listening to and watching Hindu
spiritual content. It has no ads, no sign-up and no tracking. This policy
explains exactly what happens to your information.

## 1. Information that stays on your phone

These are stored only on your device and are never sent to us:

- Favourites, reading progress and reading history
- Naam Jaap counts, streaks and Sadhana routines
- Meditation and manifestation boards
- Reminders, recently watched videos, language, theme and text-size settings

## 2. Information that leaves your phone

| Data | Why | Shared? |
|---|---|---|
| **Push-notification token** (Firebase Cloud Messaging) | To deliver festival and daily reminders | No |
| **Anonymous app ID** (Firebase anonymous sign-in, no name, email or phone) | Lets the app file an integrity report securely | No |
| **Integrity report** — the address of a downloaded file that looked damaged, the app version and platform | So a broken file can be fixed. It never includes anything about you | No |

Books, audio and videos are downloaded from public hosts (GitHub,
archive.org, YouTube). Those sites receive your request like any ordinary web
visit, under their own privacy policies.

All data sent by the app is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

## 3. Emails you choose to send

Consultation, seva and book requests open **your own email app** with a draft.
Nothing is sent unless you press send there.

## 4. What we never do

- We never sell or share your information with third parties
- We never show ads or build a profile of you
- We never read your contacts, photos, microphone or location
- Firebase Analytics is included in the app but **collection is switched off**

## 5. Delete your data

You can delete everything at any time, from inside the app:

1. Open HinduMania.
2. Tap **Menu → Privacy & your data**.
3. Tap **Delete my data** and confirm.

This immediately removes all data on your phone (favourites, progress,
streaks, routines, boards, reminders and settings), deletes your push token,
and deletes the anonymous app ID from our server. Integrity reports contain no
personal information; they are kept only until the damaged file is fixed.

Uninstalling the app also removes all data stored on your phone.

## 6. Children

HinduMania is intended for users aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly
collect personal information from children.
